==================
Using BAGLE Models
==================


.. automodule:: model

For a more detailed explanation of how BAGLE models are structured and
how you can add your own new models and parameterizations, see
:ref:`Making Your Own Microlens Models <new_models>`.

Static geometry plots of PSBL, BSPL, and BSBL events (Keplerian
orbits plus parallax, and parallax with no orbital motion) are shown
in `example_plot_PSBL_BSBL.ipynb
<https://github.com/MovingUniverseLab/BAGLE_Microlensing/blob/dev/docs/notebooks/example_plot_PSBL_BSBL.ipynb>`_.
